## Description (max 4000 tekens)

> Raak! is the classic sea battle game, made for kids and the whole family. Shuffle your fleet, set it up in secret and take turns shooting at the other player's sea. Hit? Then you go again right away!
>
> TOGETHER ON ONE DEVICE
> Play against each other on a single iPhone or iPad. The device goes from hand to hand, with a friendly pass screen that keeps both seas secret — nobody can peek.
>
> OR AGAINST THE COMPUTER
> Three opponents, each with their own character: Dommel just fires anywhere, Robbie aims pretty well and Professor Punt almost never misses. And they play fair: the computer doesn't know where your boats are and genuinely searches, just like a person would. So the game grows along with your child.
>
> A FLEET WITH CHARACTER
> Five boats with their own name and color, from the little Dinghy to the big Captain's Ship. Three seas to choose from: small for the youngest players, large for real captains. Setting up your fleet is one tap on the shuffle button — no dragging required.
>
> MADE FOR LITTLE HANDS
> Big squares, cheerful colors, a personal avatar for every player, a burst for every direct hit and confetti for the winner. Meanwhile plenty of aiming, counting and reasoning is going on — but we just call it playing.
>
> SAFE AND CALM
> No ads, no accounts, no internet required. Everything stays on your own device. Period.
>
> START FREE, UNLOCK ONCE
> For free you play together on one device or solo against Robbie, in the classic theme. The one-time Family Version unlocks all three opponents, the Candy, Ocean and Night color themes, and per-player statistics — with trophies, records and win streaks. One purchase for the whole family, through Family Sharing. And it sits behind a parental gate, so little fingers can't buy anything by accident.
>
> Ready, aim… Raak!
